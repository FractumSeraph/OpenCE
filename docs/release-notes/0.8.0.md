## What's new in 0.8.0 (Unreleased)

**Text chat.** Chupathingy players can talk in games hosted by Chupathingy, as on Halo PC: T says a line to everyone, Y to your team, Enter sends and Escape cancels. Lines show at the lower left in the HUD's own font, names in their team's color, and fade after a few seconds. H shows the recent lines and the players, where M mutes someone for you alone. On a controller, D-pad up picks a quick line and D-pad down says it. Chat only passes between Chupathingy copies and servers; OpenCE players in the same game see nothing different, and nothing that is said is written down anywhere.
- Server messages arrive in the chat too: a dedicated server's `sv_say` (or Say on its control panel) shows as a "Server" line to every Chupathingy player.
- Blocked words are starred out by default (`chat.filter`: mask, hide or show), from the blocked names list and your own `chat_filter.txt`. Nobody is ever kicked for a word.
- Turn it off with `chat.enabled = false`, show only your team with `chat.show = "team"`, or hide names with `chat.streamer_mode`. The keys are in Controls Setup.
- Hosting: `network.chat` (on, team or off) and `network.chat_filter`.

**Follow camera.** When the wait to respawn is long (a game type with a long respawn time, out of lives, or odd man out), your camera follows a living teammate from behind once the death camera is done, as in Halo 2 and 3; in a game without teams it follows your killer or the leader. LB and RB (Q and E, or the mouse wheel) switch who, and a small "FOLLOWING" line says who it is. A short respawn keeps the original death camera. It is your view only, so it works in any game, and each split screen player has their own. `display.follow_camera` (long_waits, always or off) and `display.follow_camera_after` (seconds, 5 by default) choose when.

**Dedicated server**
- The log is now `logs/debug.txt` in the data folder (`/data/logs/debug.txt` in Docker), not `/data/debug.txt`: update anything that reads the old path. No file is left at the old path. The Docker image keeps its cache in `/data/cache` (`HALO_CACHE_ROOT`), and `HALO_LOG_ROOT` moves the log.
- `sv_say`, `sv_chat [on|team|off]`, `sv_mute <player> [minutes]` and `sv_unmute`. Moderators can mute a player's chat from the in-game Moderation screen or the control panel; the muted player's game tells them.

**Changes**
- Sharper text looks more like Halo: the high-res text and Online Games now use Cyborg Interface Gothic, a free font made for this by fizzy to match Halo's interface font (SIL Open Font License). Thanks, fizzy.
- Button prompts follow the keyboard: after you use the keyboard or mouse, the HUD names the bound key ("Hold [E] to pick up", "Hold Tab for score") and the Xbox menus' A/B/X/Y prompts show key caps, as Halo PC did. Pressing a controller button switches back. `input.keyboard_prompts = false` keeps the controller's buttons. Thanks to OpenRift for the idea.
- Use your own font for the sharp text and Online Games: put a `.ttf` or `.otf` in the new `data/fonts` folder beside `maps` and set `display.high_res_font = "YourFont.ttf"` (`display.high_res_font_large` sets one for the large text only). A file that can't be read is noted in the log and the built-in font is used.
- The game's logs have a folder of their own: `logs/`, beside `maps` (`logs/debug.txt`; on Windows also `halo.log`; and crash reports waiting to be sent, which on Windows were beside `halo.exe`). For a bug report, attach what is in `logs/`. Nothing is moved: an old `debug.txt` beside `maps` is left as it was, and crash reports still waiting in the old place are sent from there. New settings `paths.logs` and `paths.cache` put the logs and the cache elsewhere.
- Video Setup is shorter: Frame Rate Limit and Smooth Motion are one Frame Rate row, where 30 (Xbox) keeps the game's own frames and the others blend between them. Instant Aim moved to config.toml alone (`display.direct_camera`, still on by default). Thanks to OpenRift for the suggestion.
- Audio Setup can choose the output device the sound plays on (Output Device), or leave it on Default to follow the system's. It switches as soon as you press OK. In config.toml: `audio.device`. Thanks to MobSlicer152 for the idea.
- Chupathingy keeps its own saves on Windows and Linux; your profiles and game types are copied over the first time.
- Online Games looks like the Xbox's menus: their fonts, glow and panels, larger rows, the Xbox's buttons for a controller (key caps for the keyboard), and a tag on every game's map for its family (XBX, HCE, HMD, GBX). A game with a password shows a lock, and the ping column shows the round trip to a host you are connected to.
- Online Games shows each game's ping before you join. Hosts on this version answer a quick ping (it never takes a slot); for older hosts you get an estimate from the game's region, shown as `~85`. Sorting by Ping lists measured pings first, then estimates. No pings with online play off, and `network.ping_games = false` turns direct pings off.
- The Halo PC menus are called Gearbox now: `display.menus = "gearbox"` (`"pc"` still works).
- Discord now shows Chupathingy for players whose settings still named the old application.

**Fixes**
- When a Custom Edition map is too big for the tag cache, debug.txt now says plainly how much room it needs and how much there is.
- A Custom Edition map the game can't play now says why on screen (for example, that Custom Edition's bitmaps.map is missing from maps_ce, or that the map is too big for the tag cache), in place of the damaged disc message.
- Android: Link Profile works again after the first run, and your finished games are confirmed on the game list. The game no longer refused its own player key.
- Android: the menus' Quit closes the game; it did nothing before.
